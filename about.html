My Story

Bringing doula care and naturopathy together

I started Well Beyond Now because I kept meeting women who felt let down by fragmented care — a midwife here, a specialist there, nobody looking at the whole picture, and nobody in the room simply to hold their hand.

As a qualified doula and naturopath, I work with women across every stage: preparing the body and mind for conception and pregnancy, providing continuous, hands-on support through labour, and guiding recovery through the fourth trimester and beyond.

My approach blends evidence-informed naturopathic care with the emotional, physical and practical support of traditional doula work — because you deserve both.

My Philosophy

What guides my work

◒

Whole-Woman Care

Your body, mind, hormones and history are all connected. I look at the whole picture, not just the symptom in front of me.

✿

Informed, Not Instructed

You know your body best. I share evidence and options so you can make decisions that feel right for you — never one-size-fits-all advice.

☾

Continuity of Care

From your first consult to your postpartum check-ins, I stay with you — because birth doesn't end when the baby arrives.
